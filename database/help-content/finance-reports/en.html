System rules

  • Choose a date range; the report takes the invoices issued in it (not drafts, not cancelled).
  • Each currency is a separate row; currencies are never added together.
  • Credit notes and payments of those same invoices (whatever their date) are included, so "outstanding" is what those invoices still owe today.
  • Everything is read from stored amounts (including final rounding).
ColumnMeaning
Grossinvoice grand totals, tax included
Credit notescredit notes of these invoices
Netgross − credit notes
Taxtax on these invoices
Paid / Outstandingpaid so far / net − paid

Example (IRR): gross 500 million, credit notes 20, net 480, tax 40, paid 350, outstanding 130 million → 130 million still to collect.

Finance tip: sales are not cash. "Net" is what you sold; "paid" is the money that actually arrived. Track outstanding ÷ net every month; if it rises, collection is getting weaker.
