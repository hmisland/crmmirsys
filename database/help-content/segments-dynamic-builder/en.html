Marketing › Segments › New dynamic segment. Each condition is field + condition + value, e.g. "City · is · Tabriz".

AND and OR

  • Conditions sit in groups. Inside a group choose All conditions (AND) or Any condition (OR).
  • Between groups choose matching all groups (AND) or any group (OR).
  • This expresses things like (A AND B) OR (C AND D).

Available fields

Name, email, mobile (only for users with access to contacts), status, gender, city, province, country, source, job title, contact created (before / after / on / last N days), date of birth, birthday month, contact group, tag, company, interest, and marketing opt-out (SMS or email).

Examples

  • New contacts: Status is Active AND Contact created in the last 30 days.
  • Birthdays this month: Birthday month is (current month) AND Status is Active.
  • Two cities: one group with Any condition: City is Tabriz, City is Shiraz.
  • A tag outside a group: Tag has "VIP" AND Contact group is not in "Partners".

System rules

  • Only the listed fields and conditions are accepted; at least one condition is required; up to 10 groups and 50 conditions.
  • Preview shows the matching count (and contacts, if you may view them) without saving.
  • "Last N days" is counted from today every time — no refresh needed.

Tip: new segments start with "Status is Active" — keep it unless you really want inactive contacts.
