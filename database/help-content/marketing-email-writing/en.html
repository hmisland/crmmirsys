A structure that works

  1. Subject (under ~50 characters): concrete and honest. "{{first_name}}, your 15% discount ends Friday" beats "NEWSLETTER #12".
  2. First line: greet by name and say why you are writing.
  3. One message, one action: one offer or piece of news, and one clear link or button.
  4. Short: 3–6 short paragraphs. People scan on their phones.
  5. Signature: your business name and how to reach you.

System rules

  • Only the listed variables work (see "Email marketing basics"); an unknown one is refused.
  • If a contact has no first name, {{first_name}} becomes empty — write "Hello {{first_name}}," so it still reads well.
  • Marketing emails always get an unsubscribe link at the bottom. To place it yourself, put {{unsubscribe_url}} in a link.
  • The HTML body is sent as written; the preview is shown safely in an isolated frame.

Example:

Subject: {{first_name}}, a new service for you

<p>Hello {{first_name}},</p><p>From next week we also offer home delivery in your city.</p><p><a href="https://yourdomain.com/delivery">See how it works</a></p><p>— The {{organization}} team</p>

Tip: avoid images that contain all the text — many email apps block images by default, and your message would be blank.
