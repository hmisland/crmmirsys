Before your first invoice, open Finance › Settings. These settings are the defaults for every document that follows.

System rules

  • Entity type: legal (the company's legal name is required) or natural person (first name required). This name is printed on invoices.
  • Tax profile: the default tax for new documents. Without one, documents are calculated untaxed.
  • Invoice prefix: e.g. INV-, giving INV-00001, INV-00002… A number is assigned only when an invoice is issued; numbers are consecutive and never reused.
  • Pricing mode: "tax exclusive" adds tax on top of prices; "tax inclusive" means the entered price already contains the tax.
  • Final invoice rounding: OFF by default (see "Final invoice rounding").
  • Only users allowed to edit finance settings can save this page.

Example: "Novaran Ltd" is a legal entity with prefix NV- and 10% tax, tax exclusive. An item priced 1,000,000 IRR becomes 1,000,000 + 100,000 tax = 1,100,000 IRR, and the first issued invoice is NV-00001.

Finance tip — currencies don't add up: 100 USD + 5,000,000 IRR is not a meaningful number, because exchange rates change every day. That is why every total and report is shown per currency and currencies are never added together.
