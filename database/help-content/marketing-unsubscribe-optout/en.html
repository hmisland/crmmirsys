Sending marketing messages to people who asked you to stop is illegal in many countries and damages your sender reputation. The CRM keeps one opt-out list per organization: Marketing › Opt-outs.

How addresses get on the list

ReasonSource
Unsubscribedthe link in a marketing email, or the provider's unsubscribe event
Bouncedthe address does not exist (Brevo hard bounce)
Spam reportthe recipient marked your email as spam
Blockedthe mobile number blocked promotional SMS at the operator
Added manuallya user added the number or email

System rules

  • Marketing campaigns skip every address on the list — at review, and again at the moment of sending.
  • Transactional (service) messages skip bounced, spam-reported and blocked addresses, but not "unsubscribed".
  • Unsubscribe links are signed and keep working even if the Marketing module is later switched off.
  • Adding or removing entries needs the "manage opt-outs" permission; users without access to contacts see addresses masked.
  • Removing an entry means that address can receive marketing again — only do it if the person asked.

Never re-add unsubscribed people through a new group or import. The list is checked by address, so they stay protected — and that is intentional.
