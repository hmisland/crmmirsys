Contact sync turns the customers of a shop into CRM contacts, so you can see them, follow them up and reach them in campaigns. It is off until you switch it on, separately for each shop.

Switch it on

  1. Go to Organization settings › Manage integrations and click Contact sync on the shop’s connection card.
  2. Tick Create and complete CRM contacts from this shop's customers.
  3. Choose a Contact group, or type a name under Or create a new contact group.
  4. Optionally add Tags (comma separated, up to 10) and a Source for new contacts (the shop’s domain by default).
  5. Check the Field mapping and click Save settings.
  6. Click Sync contacts now. It first fetches the shop’s latest orders, then creates or completes contacts. After that, the background task keeps doing it for new orders.

Switching it on needs permission to create and edit contacts. New contacts belong to the user who switched it on. No message or campaign is sent.

How a customer is matched

  • The customer’s email and Iranian mobile are looked up among existing contacts.
  • Found → the contact is Matched: only its empty fields are filled (Completed); existing details are never overwritten. The tags are added; the group is set only if the contact has none.
  • Not found → a new contact is Created. It needs a first name and a valid email or mobile; otherwise the customer is Skipped.
  • Email and mobile point to different contacts, or to several contacts → Conflict: nothing is changed.

Skipped and conflicting customers are listed under Customers needing attention with the reason. Fix the contacts in the CRM (for example merge duplicates or correct a number); the customer is checked again when it changes in the shop, or when you save the settings.

Field mapping

Each contact field is filled from the billing details of the customer’s latest order. Choose Do not import to leave a field alone. Map at least the email or the mobile, otherwise nobody can be identified.

WooCommerce has one phone field. Choose how it is used:

  • Automatic — a mobile number goes to Mobile, a landline to Landline; numbers of unclear kind are not imported.
  • Mobile only — other numbers are ignored.
  • Always as Landline.

Why fewer contacts than orders? One contact is made per customer. A customer with five orders is one contact. Guest customers (no shop account) are included, identified by the email and phone of their orders.
