Order of calculation

  1. Each line: quantity × unit price − line discount (percentage or fixed amount) = line total.
  2. Subtotal: the sum of all lines.
  3. Invoice discount: one fixed amount, at most the subtotal.
  4. Tax: on the amount after discounts.
  5. Grand total = subtotal − invoice discount + tax.

Precision rules

ValueMaximum decimals
IRR amount3 (rials are never rounded to whole numbers automatically)
Other currenciesthe currency's precision (e.g. USD 2)
Unit pricecurrency precision + 2 (IRR 5, USD 4)
Quantity and percentage discount4
  • A more precise number is refused with a message; it is never silently cut.
  • "1,000" is accepted. "1e3", negative numbers, discounts above 100% or above the line amount are refused.
  • Rounding is half-up: 1.0005 IRR → 1.001.

Example (10% tax, tax exclusive):

  • Line 1: 3 × 250,000 with a 10% discount = 675,000
  • Line 2: 2 × 100,000 with a 20,000 discount = 180,000
  • Subtotal 855,000 − invoice discount 5,000 = 850,000; tax 85,000; grand total 935,000

In tax-inclusive mode, a price of 1,100,000 with 10% tax means 1,000,000 net + 100,000 tax.

Finance tip: discounts apply before tax, so they reduce the tax too. Tax you collect is not your income; you hold it for the tax authority.
