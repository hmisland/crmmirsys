A campaign is one message (SMS or email) sent to one audience. Everything happens under Marketing.

The pieces

  • Campaign: name, channel (email), provider (Sender or Brevo), subject, HTML body, optional plain-text version.
  • Audience: selected contacts, a contact group, contacts with a tag, a saved segment, or all active contacts.
  • Template: a reusable subject and body (Marketing › Templates), loaded into a new campaign.
  • Personalization: {{first_name}}, {{last_name}}, {{full_name}}, {{company}}, {{email}}, {{phone}}, {{organization}}, {{unsubscribe_url}}.

System rules

  • A campaign is saved as a draft; the review page shows the number of recipients, skipped contacts (no address, invalid, opted out, duplicates) and a personalized preview.
  • Approve and send needs the "send campaigns" permission. The recipient count you confirm must still be correct — if contacts changed meanwhile, review again.
  • Sending runs in the background (the CRM's scheduled worker, or while the campaign page is open). You can close the page.
  • Every person receives a separate email; nobody sees other recipients.
  • An unknown variable such as {{coupon}} is refused — it would otherwise be sent as-is.

Tip: always use Send a test to yourself before approving. Read it on your phone — most emails are opened there.
