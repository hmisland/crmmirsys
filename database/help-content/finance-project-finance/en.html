Formulas (each currency separately)

  • Net revenue = issued invoices attributed to the project, excluding tax and minus their share of credit notes. Drafts, proformas, installments and the contract value are not revenue.
  • Actual cost = the net of expenses allocated to the project (void ones excluded).
  • Profit = net revenue − actual cost (can be negative).
  • Margin = profit ÷ net revenue × 100 (only when revenue is above zero).
  • Budget per expense category × currency. Remaining = budget − actual; variance = actual − budget (positive means over budget).

Seeing profit requires several permissions together (invoices, credit notes, expenses and budget), so no figure leaks to someone who must not see it.

Example: net revenue 200 million, cost 150 million → profit 50 million, margin 25%. Labor budget 80 million, actual 92 million → variance +12 million (15% over budget).

Finance tip: margin differs from markup: 50 on 200 is a 25% margin but a 33% markup on cost. Watch budget variance early, not at the end of the project.
