When a contact is linked to a shop customer, the contact page shows a Shop orders panel with that customer’s orders from every connected shop.

What the panel shows

ColumnMeaning
ShopThe connection the order came from.
OrderThe order number in WooCommerce.
DateWhen the order was placed.
StatusCompleted, Processing, On hold, Pending payment, Cancelled, Refunded, Failed or Draft — as in the shop.
TotalThe order total in the shop’s currency.

When is a contact linked to a customer?

  • When contact sync creates or matches the contact (see “Turning shop customers into CRM contacts”).
  • When the order’s email matches the email of an existing contact.

If the panel says No shop orders for this contact, the contact is not linked to any shop customer yet, or the customer’s orders have not been imported (for example orders older than 30 days — see “Importing older WooCommerce orders”).

Who sees it

Everyone who can view the contact, as long as the WooCommerce module is active for the organization. The panel is read-only: orders are changed in the shop and arrive with the next sync.

Tip: the same orders power the “Shop purchases” conditions of Marketing segments, for example “bought product X in the last 90 days”.
