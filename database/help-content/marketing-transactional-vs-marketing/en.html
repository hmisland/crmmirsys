Why these limits

Marking a promotion as "transactional" would send it to people who unsubscribed. The CRM checks the type when the campaign is saved and again at approval, so the rule cannot be bypassed by changing one field.

Rule of thumb: if the message would still make sense to someone who never wants offers from you, it is transactional. If it promotes, it is marketing.

System emails such as password reset and account verification are separate from Marketing and are not affected by these settings.
