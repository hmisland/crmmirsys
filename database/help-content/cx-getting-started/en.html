Customer satisfaction sends a short survey to customers automatically after an event, such as a completed shop order, and collects a score and an optional comment. Dissatisfied answers become follow-ups for your team.

Before you start: the Customer satisfaction module must be active, and at least one delivery channel (SMS, email or Bale) must be set up in Messaging providers; invitations are sent through Marketing’s channels. For automatic surveys after orders you also need a connected WooCommerce shop.

Create a survey

  1. Open Customer satisfaction in the sidebar and click New survey.
  2. Enter a Survey name and choose the Type:
    • CSAT — satisfaction from 1 to 5.
    • NPS — likelihood to recommend from 0 to 10.
    • CES — how easy it was, from 1 to 7.
  3. Write the Main question. Optionally tick Also ask for a written comment, and edit the intro and thank-you texts.
  4. Under Send when choose An order is completed for automatic sending, or Not automatic.
  5. Set the Delay before sending (default 60 minutes, up to 30 days) and how long the link is valid (default 14 days, up to 90).
  6. Choose a Preferred channel or Automatic. If the preferred channel is not available for a customer, the first available one is used.
  7. Optionally write the Invitation message with the placeholders :name, :order and :link; empty uses the default text.
  8. Set the status to Active and click Save survey.

What happens after an order

  • When an order becomes completed, one invitation per active survey is scheduled after the delay. An order gets each survey only once.
  • Orders completed more than 7 days ago (for example in an import of older orders) never start a survey.
  • Each invitation has its own single-use link. Once answered, or after it expires, it cannot be used again.
  • If no channel is available, the invitation shows Waiting for a channel and is tried again every hour; after 7 days without a channel it expires.

Tip: before switching a survey on, send yourself a test invitation (see “Sending a test survey invitation”) to see the message and the survey page exactly as customers will.
