Statuses per recipient

StatusMeaning
Queuedwaiting to be sent
Accepted by providerKavenegar, Sender or Brevo took the message. This is not delivery.
Deliveredthe provider reported delivery (Kavenegar report, Brevo event)
Failedthe provider refused, with its message shown (no credit, invalid number, limit reached…)
Skippednot sent on purpose: invalid address or opted out
Cancelledthe campaign was cancelled before this message was sent

Delivery reports

  • SMS (Kavenegar): delivery is fetched automatically for 48 hours after sending.
  • Email via Brevo: delivered, bounced, spam and unsubscribe events arrive through the webhook.
  • Email via Sender: only "accepted" is reported to the CRM; see delivery and opens in Sender's own logs.

Retry failed messages

  • Network problems and rate limits are retried automatically (up to 3 attempts, with a delay).
  • Retry failed messages queues the remaining failures again — fix the cause first (e.g. charge credit).
  • A message that may already have gone out (the provider did not answer in time) is marked so and is never retried, to avoid sending twice.

Tip: the dashboard shows the last 30 days per channel. Many failures or bounces after a send mean your list or domain needs attention.
