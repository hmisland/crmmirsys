In a new campaign choose Recipients › Saved segment, or press Use in a campaign on the segment page.

Estimate vs. final count

  • The review page shows an estimate: contacts matching the segment now, and how many are eligible after removing those without a valid address, opted-out contacts and duplicate numbers/emails.
  • When you approve, the segment is calculated again. If the number changed meanwhile, approval is refused — review and approve again.
  • The final number of recipients is fixed at approval and shown on the campaign page.

Large segments

Above a set size (by default 2,000 contacts), recipients are prepared in the background after approval. The campaign shows "Preparing" and then starts sending by itself.

If the segment changes or is deleted

  • At approval the campaign keeps a copy of the segment's definition. Editing or deleting the segment afterwards does not change a campaign that is already approved.
  • A draft that uses a deleted segment cannot be approved — choose another audience.

Rules that still apply

  • Opt-outs, bounces and blocks are always skipped.
  • Transactional messages cannot use segments — only individually selected contacts.
  • Using a segment never shows contact details to users who may not view contacts.
