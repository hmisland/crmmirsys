What a follow-up is

A follow-up is a planned contact or action with a customer: a call, email, message, meeting, task, reminder, note or custom type, with a priority (low, normal, high, urgent), a due date and a responsible person. It can be related to a contact, deal, contract or project, and appears on their pages.

Working with follow-ups

  • Follow-ups → New creates one; the list can be filtered and searched.
  • On a follow-up: Complete, Cancel or Reschedule to a new date.
  • The dashboard shows your next follow-ups.

Automatic follow-ups (rules)

With the Sales module, Follow-ups → Rules creates a follow-up automatically when a deal reaches a stage — for example “call the customer 2 days after the proposal”. A rule can add a reminder some minutes before the due time; the responsible person then receives a reminder email.

For the administrator: reminder emails need the CRM mail settings and the scheduler cron (app/bin/scheduler.php, every minute).
