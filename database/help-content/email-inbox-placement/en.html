When the CRM shows Accepted by provider and Sender or Brevo shows Delivered, the email has reached the recipient's mailbox. Which folder it lands in is Gmail's (or Yahoo's, Outlook's…) decision.

Promotions is not Spam

  • Gmail's Promotions tab is part of the inbox. Newsletters and offers sent through email services usually land there — that is normal.
  • Spam means the receiver does not trust the message or the sender.

What helps

  • Authentication: SPF, DKIM and DMARC all passing (the most important factor).
  • Short, personal text with your contact's name, one clear link, few images, no ALL CAPS or "!!!".
  • Only to people who expect it: never send to bought lists; remove bounced addresses (the CRM does this automatically).
  • A working unsubscribe link (the CRM adds one to every marketing email).
  • Service messages (order confirmation, appointment reminder) as Transactional — they usually reach the main inbox.
  • Time: a new domain builds reputation over weeks. Start with smaller sends to engaged contacts.
  • Ask recipients to drag your email to Primary or reply to it — Gmail learns from that.

Warning signs: many bounces or spam reports damage your domain. Check the campaign results and the opt-out list after each send.
