Business rules

  • Four-eyes by default: members can prepare drafts; someone with "send campaigns" approves them.
  • Contact privacy: without access to Contacts you can still work with groups, tags and segments, but you never see names, numbers or emails — previews use placeholders like [first_name] and lists are masked.
  • Test limits: test SMS and test emails share one limit — by default 5 per user per 10 minutes and 20 per organization per hour.
  • Module access: without the Marketing module the pages are unavailable; without the Email Marketing module email cannot be configured or sent. Hiding a button is never the only protection — the server checks every action.
  • Opt-outs are always honoured for marketing; unsubscribe links work even if the module is switched off later.
  • No double sending or charging: every recipient is sent once per campaign, and retries never charge SMS credit twice.
  • Organizations are separate: no campaign, segment, contact or setting is ever visible to another organization.
