Marketing › Transactional lists the single messages that other modules sent through Marketing’s channels, for example customer satisfaction survey invitations. Campaign messages are not listed here; see the campaign and Message history for those.

Columns and filters

  • Recipient, Channel (SMS, email, Bale), Source (which module and purpose sent it), Requested and Sent time.
  • Filter by search text, channel, status and source.

Statuses

StatusMeaning
SendingBeing handed to the provider now.
Accepted by providerThe provider took the message. This is not yet proof of delivery.
Will retryThe provider was temporarily unavailable; it is sent again automatically.
FailedThe provider rejected it, or sending was interrupted. The reason is shown.
Not sentSkipped on purpose: invalid address, the recipient opted out of this channel, or the request was incomplete.

The Delivery column shows whether the message reached the phone or inbox, only when the provider reports it. “Not reported” means no report has arrived yet. Examples of reports: Delivered, Not delivered, Bounced, Blocked by recipient.

Why a message was not re-sent

If sending was interrupted midway, the message may already have gone out. To avoid sending it twice, it is not re-sent automatically and is shown with that reason.

Tip: test survey invitations appear with the source “Customer satisfaction survey (test)”, so you can tell them apart from real ones.
