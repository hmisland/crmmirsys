Email campaigns are sent through your organization's own account at an email service (Sender or Brevo). The CRM never sends marketing email from a shared server.

The four steps

  1. Module enabled
  2. Provider account
  3. Domain verified
  4. Connected and tested
  1. Module: the platform administrator enables Marketing and Email Marketing for your organization. Without them the email cards in settings are shown muted with "Module not purchased".
  2. Provider account: sign up at Sender (see "Signing up for Sender") or Brevo, and create an API key.
  3. Domain: add the SPF, DKIM and DMARC records the provider gives you to your domain's DNS (see "Verifying your sender domain").
  4. Connect: Organization settings › Messaging providers → enter the key, sender email and sender name → Save and test connection → Send test email.

System rules

  • Only users who may edit organization settings see this page.
  • The API key is stored encrypted and is never shown again; only its last 4 characters appear.
  • A successful connection test does not prove your domain may send — that depends on the DNS records.
  • Test messages are limited (by default 5 per user per 10 minutes, 20 per organization per hour).

Tip: send your first test to your own Gmail address. If it arrives, your setup is complete; where it lands (Primary, Promotions, Spam) is a separate topic.
