Modules & Features (Organization settings › Modules › Modules & Features) lists every module the platform offers, grouped by purpose, and shows whether each one is active for your organization. It is visible to users who can edit organization settings.

The groups

  • Sales & customers — Sales, Follow-ups, Contact history, Appointments.
  • Online shop — WooCommerce integration.
  • Marketing & customer experience — Marketing, Email marketing, Bale messaging, Customer satisfaction.
  • Projects & contracts, Finance (Finance, Wallet), Communication & support (Internal mail, Notifications, Support, Knowledge base & files).

What the statuses mean

StatusMeaningWhat you can do
ActiveThe module is on for your organization.Open it.
AvailableOffered by the platform but not on for you.Request activation.
Request pendingYour request is waiting for the platform team.Wait; you are notified.
Request rejectedYour last request was not approved; the reason is shown if one was given.Request again.

Dependencies and add-ons

Some modules need another module first. The card says, for example, Requires Wallet with that module’s status. Notifications requires Wallet; Email marketing and Bale messaging require Marketing and are listed right under it. A module cannot be activated while a module it requires is off, so request the required one first, or both.

Good to know

  • The page shows no prices and asks for no payment.
  • Whether a member of your team can use an active module is managed separately on the Modules page (member access).
  • For WooCommerce, the card lists what the integration does; when active, Open takes you to the shop connections.
