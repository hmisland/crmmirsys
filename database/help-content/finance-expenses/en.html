System rules

  • An expense is a positive, actual cost (excluding recoverable tax) with a currency, category (labor, procurement, contractor, shipping, services, other), date, payee and receipts.
  • Allocating to a project is optional; only allocated expenses count in a project's cost, budget and profit. A contract reference is informational only and is used in no calculation.
  • Statuses: recorded and void (with a reason). There is no delete.
  • Editing only while recorded and dated in an open period. After the first correction, the original is frozen.
  • Correction: an increase or decrease row; the expense's net never goes below zero.

Example: 15 million IRR to a contractor for the "Branch installation" project. The contractor's bill later becomes 13 million; you record a "decrease 2 million" correction and the net is 13 million, with both records kept.

Finance tip: consistent categories make analysis possible ("contractor costs rose 20% on last month"). Always attach the receipt.
