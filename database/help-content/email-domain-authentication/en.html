Gmail and Yahoo reject or hide email from domains that are not authenticated. Your email provider gives you three records to add to your domain's DNS.

RecordType / NameWhat it does
DKIMCNAME, e.g. sender._domainkeysigns every email so receivers know it really comes from you
SPFTXT on @, starts with v=spf1lists the servers allowed to send for your domain
DMARCTXT on _dmarctells receivers what to do with mail that fails the checks

Adding them in cPanel

  1. cPanel › Zone Editor › Manage for your domain.
  2. DKIM: Add Record › CNAME, with the name and value from your provider.
  3. SPF: filter by TXT and edit the existing v=spf1 record — add the provider's include:… before ~all. If no SPF exists, add one TXT record.
  4. DMARC: Add Record › "DMARC" (or TXT) with name _dmarc and value v=DMARC1; p=none;.
  5. Wait a few minutes to a few hours, then press the provider's "check records" button.

Never create a second SPF record. A domain must have exactly one v=spf1 record; two break all your email, including normal mailboxes.

Example (Sender): existing SPF v=spf1 +a +mx ~all becomes v=spf1 +a +mx include:sendersrv.com ~all; DKIM is sender._domainkey → dkim.sendersrv.com.

Tip: p=none only monitors. Once everything has worked for a few weeks you can make DMARC stricter (quarantine), but there is no need to start there.
