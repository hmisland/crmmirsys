The Customer satisfaction page shows how your surveys are doing and which customers need attention.

Per survey

  • Invitations — how many were sent.
  • Responses — how many customers answered.
  • Average score — on the survey’s own scale.

How answers are classified

TypeNegativeMiddlePositive
CSAT (1–5)1–2 Dissatisfied3 Neutral4–5 Satisfied
NPS (0–10)0–6 Detractor7–8 Passive9–10 Promoter
CES (1–7)1–3 Dissatisfied4 Neutral5–7 Satisfied

Following up dissatisfied customers

Every Dissatisfied or Detractor answer appears under Dissatisfied customers to follow up, with the score, the comment and the order. Contact the customer, solve the problem, then click Mark as handled. Handled follow-ups leave the list; the response itself stays in the results.

Recent responses and invitations

Recent responses lists the latest answers with score, rating and comment.

The invitation statuses tell you where each one is:

  • Scheduled — waiting for its delay to pass.
  • Waiting for a channel — no channel can reach this customer yet; retried hourly; after 7 days without a channel it becomes Expired.
  • Sent / Answered — delivered to the channel / the customer replied.
  • Expired — the link’s validity ended without an answer.
  • Failed / Cancelled — not delivered (the reason is shown), or stopped because the survey was switched off or the module is no longer active.

Tip: who sees what: the permission to view Customer satisfaction shows results; marking follow-ups as handled and editing surveys need the permission to manage it. Delivery details of each message are in Marketing › Transactional.
