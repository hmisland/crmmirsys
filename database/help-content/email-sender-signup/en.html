Sender (sender.net) is an email service with a free plan. The CRM uses your account through its official API.

Steps

  1. Open sender.net and choose Sign up. Use a business email address of your own domain if you have one.
  2. Confirm the email Sender sends you and complete the short account profile (company name, address — required by anti-spam laws and shown in email footers).
  3. In Sender, open Settings › API access tokens and create a new token. Give it a recognizable name, e.g. "CRM".
  4. Copy the token immediately — it is long (about 1,000 characters) and Sender shows it only once.
  5. Before sending, authenticate your domain (see "Verifying your sender domain").

Rules

  • Paste the token in one piece, without spaces or line breaks. The CRM rejects a token that contains spaces.
  • Treat the token like a password: it can send email in your name. Don't share it in chat or email; if it leaks, delete it in Sender and create a new one.
  • One organization = one Sender account. Each organization in the CRM enters its own token.

Attention: deleting the token in Sender immediately stops campaigns in the CRM. Create the new token first, save it in the CRM, then delete the old one.
