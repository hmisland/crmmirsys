System rules

  • Payments are recorded only on issued invoices: amount, method (cash, bank transfer, card, other), reference, date and notes.
  • Balance = grand total − credit notes − payments. A payment cannot exceed the balance.
  • The status is calculated automatically: unpaid, partially paid, paid.
  • The payment date must not be in a closed financial period.
  • If the form is sent twice (double click), only one payment is recorded.
  • Payments cannot be edited or deleted and stay in the invoice's "Financial history"; enter them carefully.

Example: invoice of 1,100,000 IRR. On 1 October, 600,000 by bank transfer → "partially paid", balance 500,000. On 12 October, 500,000 in cash → "paid". Entering 600,000 that day is refused because it exceeds the balance.

Finance tip — collections: a sale is complete only when the money is in. Review overdue invoices every week, and record the bank reference so matching with the bank statement is easy.
