A campaign can be sent right after approval or at a date and time you choose, for example tomorrow at 10:00.

Schedule a campaign

  1. Create the campaign as usual (Marketing › Campaigns), save it and open it.
  2. In the approval section, fill in Send later (optional): a Send date and a Send time (HH:MM).
  3. Click Approve and send and confirm. The campaign shows Scheduled and the time it will be sent.

Leave both fields empty to send right after approval, as before. Approving needs the permission to send campaigns.

Rules

  • The time is in your organization’s time zone (shown under the fields).
  • The send time must be at least one minute in the future and at most one year ahead.
  • The recipients are fixed at approval. Contacts added to the segment later are not included.
  • When sending starts, opt-outs, the channel provider and module access are checked again. Someone who opted out in the meantime is skipped.
  • Sending is started by the background task (cron), so it begins within about a minute of the chosen time.

Change or stop a scheduled campaign

Until the chosen time, click Cancel campaign to stop it; nothing is sent. To change the text, audience or time, create the campaign again with the new details and schedule it.

Tip: send a test message before approving. Schedule SMS campaigns for reasonable hours: your SMS provider may block promotional messages at night.
