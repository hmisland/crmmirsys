The dashboard

Open Projects → Projects dashboard. It counts only the projects you can see: active projects, overdue projects, upcoming and late milestones and, with the Sales module, open and overdue tasks.

How dates are judged

  • Overdue project: the expected end date has passed and the project is not completed or cancelled.
  • Ending soon / upcoming: within the next 14 days.
  • Late milestone: still planned after its date. Milestones of completed or cancelled projects are never late.
  • Overdue task: not completed, cancelled or rejected, and its due date has passed.

Projects needing attention lists them most urgent first, with the reason; Projects with overdue tasks links to each project’s Kanban board.

Delay emails

When a project passes its expected end date, or a planned milestone passes its date, the project manager receives one email listing the newly late items. Each item is sent once per date; if you set a new date and it is missed again, a new email is sent.

For the administrator: emails need the CRM mail settings and the scheduler cron (app/bin/scheduler.php, every minute).
