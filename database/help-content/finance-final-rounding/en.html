Sometimes an invoice total has decimals (e.g. 1,000,000.4 IRR) and you want the customer to pay a round number. This feature does it transparently.

System rules

  • The organization default is in Finance › Settings and is OFF initially.
  • When issuing, a "Round the payable" option is shown next to the Issue button, set to that default; the issuer can change it for that invoice.
  • IRR rounds to a whole rial; other currencies to their own precision (USD 2 decimals). Method: half-up.
  • The invoice records "Total before rounding", the "Rounding adjustment" (positive or negative) and the final grand total.
  • The decision is taken once, at issue, and never recalculated. Earlier invoices are not changed.
  • Payments, balance, credit notes and reports all use the final amount; the adjustment is counted exactly once.

Example: 1,000,000.4 → 1,000,000 (adjustment −0.4). 2,500.5 → 2,501 (+0.5). USD 10.555 → 10.56. After issue, a payment of 1,000,000 settles the first invoice completely.

Finance tip: don't tweak line prices to get a round total; that distorts tax and discount figures. A recorded rounding adjustment is a small, visible difference, and that transparency matters in an audit.
