System rules

  • Each period has a start and end date; periods do not overlap.
  • In a closed period, no invoice can be issued, no issued invoice cancelled, and no payment, credit note or expense change can be made with a date inside it.
  • Closing waits for operations in progress, so nothing slips in at the last moment.
  • Reopening needs its own permission. Who closed or reopened, and when, stays in the "Close / reopen history" on the same page.

Example: you close September after reconciling with the bank. A payment dated 30 September arrives later: it is refused. Either record it with the actual receipt date, or, with a manager's approval, reopen the period, record it and close again.

Month-end checklist:

  1. All the month's invoices are issued.
  2. Payments are matched with the bank statement.
  3. Expenses and receipts are recorded.
  4. The month's financial report is reviewed.
  5. The period is closed.
