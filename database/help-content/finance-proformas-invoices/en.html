System rules

  • A proforma is a price offer, not a debt. Statuses: draft, sent, accepted, rejected, expired, converted. Until converted it can be edited or deleted.
  • Converting creates a draft invoice with the same lines. The proforma becomes "converted", is locked, and can be converted only once.
  • Invoice: draft → issued → (cancelled). A draft can be edited or deleted freely.
  • Issuing (usually organization admins only) assigns the next number and freezes the invoice. It is refused if the invoice date is in a closed financial period.
  • An issued invoice is never edited; corrections are made with a credit note.

Example: you send a proforma for 3 licences. The customer accepts; you convert it, set the due date and issue it: INV-00007. From that moment the amount is owed to you.

Finance tip: a proforma is not revenue. Only an issued invoice creates a receivable. Always set a due date so you can spot overdue invoices.
