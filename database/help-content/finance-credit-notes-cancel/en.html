Credit note

  • Reduces what the customer owes without changing the original invoice, like a "negative invoice".
  • A reason is required. Maximum amount = invoice total − earlier credit notes.
  • Gets a consecutive number (CN-00001…). The date must not be in a closed period. It cannot be edited or deleted.
  • Effect: the balance goes down; in reports, net revenue = gross − credit notes. If the customer has already paid more than the new amount, the contract or project finance panel shows a "refund due" (the refund itself is made outside the system, e.g. by bank transfer).

Cancelling an invoice

  • Draft: can be cancelled at any time.
  • Issued: only if it has no payment and no credit note, its date is in an open period, and a reason is given. Its number is not freed, and the cancellation stays on record.

Example: invoice of 1,100,000 IRR; the customer returns 2 items worth 200,000. You record a credit note of 200,000 with the reason "2 items returned"; the invoice's effective total becomes 900,000.

Finance tip: issued financial documents are never deleted or edited; a correcting document is issued instead. That way every change stays traceable for auditors and tax.
