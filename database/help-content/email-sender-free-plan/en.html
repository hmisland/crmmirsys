Sender changes its plans from time to time. The figures below were read from Sender's official pricing page in October 2026; always check the latest limits at sender.net/pricing.

Free Forever plan (October 2026)

  • Up to 2,500 subscribers and up to 15,000 emails per month; no daily sending limit is listed.
  • Sender branding is added to emails (and to forms and landing pages).
  • 1 user seat.
  • No SMS — in the CRM, SMS goes through Kavenegar, not Sender.
  • Transactional (API) emails are included, with 1 event webhook and 1-day log retention.
  • Shared sending IP; no A/B testing; up to 10 landing pages.

How the CRM counts against it

  • Every campaign recipient is one email (the CRM sends one email per person, so nobody sees other addresses). A campaign to 3,000 contacts uses 3,000 of your monthly 15,000.
  • Test emails count too.
  • Contacts stay in the CRM; the CRM does not upload your contact list to Sender.
  • When Sender refuses because a limit is reached, those recipients show as Failed with Sender's message — use Retry failed messages after the limit resets or after upgrading.

Example: 1,200 contacts × 2 newsletters a month = 2,400 emails — well inside the free plan. 4,000 contacts × 4 campaigns = 16,000 — over the monthly limit: send less often, use a segment, or upgrade.

Tip: send to segments (people who opened or bought recently) instead of everyone. Smaller, relevant sends cost less, get better open rates and protect your domain's reputation.
