The first sync of a shop imports only the orders of the last 30 days. Customers who bought earlier are therefore not in the CRM yet. Use Import older orders to bring them in.

Steps

  1. Go to Organization settings › Manage integrations and click Contact sync on the shop’s connection card.
  2. In the Import older orders section, either choose a date under Import orders changed since, or tick From the beginning (whole shop history).
  3. Click Import older orders and confirm.

The first part is imported immediately. The background task (cron) continues with the rest in small batches, so a large shop can take a while. The section shows how far the import has got and the Oldest imported order.

Good to know

  • It is safe to repeat: orders already imported are updated, never duplicated.
  • If contact sync is switched on, contacts are created from the imported customers (guests included).
  • Old orders do not send satisfaction surveys. Only orders completed in the last 7 days can start a survey.
  • If the import seems to stop, check the Queue on the connection card and that the cron job runs every minute (see “How WooCommerce orders are synchronized”).

Tip: for a shop with years of history, start with a recent date (for example one year ago), check the result, and import the rest later.
