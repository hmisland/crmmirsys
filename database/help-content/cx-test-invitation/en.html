A test invitation lets you try a survey on yourself: a real invitation with a single-use link is sent to your own mobile or email through the normal delivery, so you see the message and the survey page as a customer would.

Send a test

  1. Open Customer satisfaction and go to Send a test invitation.
  2. Choose the Survey. A survey that is not switched on yet can also be tested.
  3. Choose Send via: a channel that is set up (SMS, email or Bale).
  4. Click Send test invitation and check your phone or inbox.

It is sent to the mobile number and email in your profile. If one is missing, the form says so; add it with Edit profile. Sending tests needs the permission to manage Customer satisfaction.

How a test differs from a real invitation

  • The message starts with [Test], the invitation shows the badge Test, and in Marketing › Transactional its source is “Customer satisfaction survey (test)”.
  • No order or customer is involved, and a failed test is not retried.
  • When you answer, the link is used up and the thank-you page appears, but no response is stored: results, averages and follow-ups do not change.
  • Up to 10 test invitations per user per hour.

If the test is not sent

  • No delivery channel is ready — set up SMS, email or Bale in Messaging providers.
  • Your profile has no valid email / Iranian mobile — complete your profile.
  • Too many test invitations — wait an hour.

Tip: for SMS, the message must fit your SMS provider’s rules. Test after every change to the invitation message.
