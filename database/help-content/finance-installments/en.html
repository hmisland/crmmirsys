On each contract page, the finance section has an installment schedule: the amount and due date of each installment.

System rules

  • An installment is only a plan: not an invoice, not revenue, not a receivable.
  • A proforma or invoice is created from an installment (one active document per installment).
  • The status follows that document: not invoiced, invoiced, partially paid, settled, void. If the due date has passed and it is not settled, it is flagged overdue.
  • Installments are added or edited only on an active contract; documents can be created while it is active or expired. Voiding requires a reason.
  • If the installments don't add up to the contract value, a warning is shown (saving is not blocked).

Example: a contract of 120 million IRR with 4 quarterly installments of 30 million. The first is invoiced and paid → "settled". The second's due date passes unpaid → "overdue".

Finance tip — cash flow: being profitable is not enough; you need to know when money arrives. The installment schedule is your forecast of incoming cash; compare it with upcoming expenses.
