Create and assign

In Tasks, press New task: title, description, due date, priority, one or more assignees and, if you like, a related deal, project or contract. Tasks can also be added from a deal or project page.

Find your tasks

  • Views: assigned to me, created by me, all tasks (organization-wide permission only) and the archive.
  • Filters: search, status and Project; the Calendar shows tasks by date.

Statuses, labels and checklists

Each organization can name its own statuses (Manage statuses); every status belongs to a fixed group — open, in progress, completed or cancelled. Labels and a checklist with progress help organize a task; comments and files are on the task page.

Reminders and repeating tasks

A task can have a reminder — shown under Reminders and, if chosen, sent by email — and can repeat every day, week or month.

For the administrator: reminders and repeating tasks need the cron modules/Sales/bin/task-reminders.php every minute.
