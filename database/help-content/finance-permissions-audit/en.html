Each permission's scope (own, team, department, whole organization) decides which documents a user sees.

Audit trail

  • The invoice's "Financial history" card: payments only with permission to view payments, credit notes only with permission to view credit notes; with user, time and the amount before → after.
  • Period closing and reopening are recorded on the periods page. Invoice cancellations stay on record with their reason.
  • An event is written together with its operation: both are saved or neither. A repeated submission creates no duplicate event.
  • Payments and credit notes cannot be edited or deleted.

Example: a "Cashier" role with invoice view and payment view/create records payments, but cannot issue or cancel invoices and does not see credit notes.

Finance tip — segregation of duties: the person who issues invoices should not be the only one recording payments and closing periods. Splitting these tasks between people reduces errors and fraud.
