When the WooCommerce module is active and a shop is connected, the dynamic segment builder (Marketing › Segments) offers a Shop purchases group of conditions. They use the orders imported from your shops, so you can target customers by what and when they bought.

Which orders count as a purchase

Only orders with status Completed or Processing count for “Has purchased”, number, total, first and last purchase. Pending payment, on-hold, failed, cancelled, refunded and draft orders never count. To find those, use Has an order with status.

All shops or one shop

Every purchase condition has a Where option: In all shops (default) or one connected shop. Totals are added as stored, so if your shops use different currencies, limit amount conditions to one shop.

Which contacts are found

A contact matches through the shop customers linked to it, by contact sync or by the order email. Customers who are not contacts yet cannot be in a segment: switch on contact sync first (see “Turning shop customers into CRM contacts”). Orders older than the first 30 days appear only after you import older orders.

Tip: combine purchase conditions with ordinary ones, for example “Last purchase more than 90 days ago” AND “City is Tabriz”, and check the preview count before using the segment in a campaign.
